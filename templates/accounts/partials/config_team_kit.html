{% comment %} Team kit management. Kits are rows rather than Constance settings, so this section posts to its own routes (team_kit_add / _edit / _make_current / _toggle_active) and each redirects back here with a message. The "current" kit is the one the team is working to get everyone into. A new season's kit is added and made current; the previous one is left as it was -- still active if riders should keep recording it, or retired. Only one kit can be current, and it must be active; the database enforces both, and the controls below say so before it has to. Counts are of RECORDED statuses only. "Hasn't answered" is left out on purpose: the honest denominator is the team, which comes from Discord roles, so any number shown would include applicants and people who have left. {% endcomment %} {# --- the current kit, where it stands ------------------------------------------------ #} {% if current_kit_entry %}
