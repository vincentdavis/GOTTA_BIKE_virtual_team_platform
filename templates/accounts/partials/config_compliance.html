Delete a member's account

For handling an erasure request from someone who cannot do it themselves. This runs exactly what their own Delete Account button runs — the next screen shows what it reaches and what it does not.

Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no way to restore the account afterwards.
