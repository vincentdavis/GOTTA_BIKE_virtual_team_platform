Delete Account

This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

There is no grace period and no way to recover the account afterwards.

What is deleted

  • Your profile — name, birth year, gender, country, timezone, email, equipment and social links
  • Your Discord sign-in link, and two-factor authentication if you set it up
  • Your Race Verified records, including the weight, height and FTP measurements on them, and the verification photos and videos you uploaded as evidence
  • Your event signups and answers to signup questions, availability responses, squad memberships, race registrations and DS assignments
  • Your API keys and any login links issued to you
  • TTT plans, ladder matchups and Google Sheets data connections you created
  • YouTube videos of yours shown on the Team Feed

What we keep

Deleting your account here does not reach everything. These are kept:

  • Your Discord server membership record — Discord ID, username, nickname, roles and join date. This tracks the Discord server itself, not your account here.
  • Your membership registration — the form you filled in when you joined, which holds your name, email, birth year, gender, country and Zwift ID.
  • Your ZwiftPower and Zwift Racing data — race results, weight, height, power and heart rate. This comes from those services and is tied to your Zwift ID, not to this account. The same is true of Strava club activities.
  • Support tickets you opened, and anything you created for the team — events, squads, availability sheets, verification reviews you carried out. These stay, with your name removed from them.
  • Page visit logs, including IP address, with your name removed.
  • Your Zwift connection is not disconnected automatically. Disconnect it from your profile first if you want that undone.

If you want any of the above removed as well, ask a team admin {% if config.DISCORD_URL and config.DISCORD_URL != "#" %} on Discord {% endif %} before deleting your account — it is much harder for us to find your data afterwards.
